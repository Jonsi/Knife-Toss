# Knife Toss GDD - Phase 2 (Polish & Expansion)

**Goal:** Increase retention, deepen gameplay with strategy, and expand monetization.

## 1. Advanced Mechanics (Future)
- **Boss Levels:** Dedicated boss mechanics (HP bars, multiple phases, specialized destruction).
- **Jitter:** Unpredictable shaking for high difficulty (if not covered by existing behaviors).

## 2. Advanced Economy & Fruits
**Fruit System:**
- **Risk-Based Spawning:** High-value fruits (Cherries, Pomegranate) placed near hazards (Metal Plates, Narrow Gaps).
- **Behavior Sync:** Rare fruits spawn only during difficult movement phases (e.g., fast spin).

**Shop & Skins:**
- **Knife Skins:** Unlockable with Coins (Tomatoes).
- **Target Skins:** Visual themes.

## 3. Metagame Features
- **Endless Mode:** Survive as many levels as possible with increasing difficulty.
- **Leaderboards:** Global/Friend scores for Endless Mode.
- **Daily Challenges:** Unique level configurations refreshed daily.
- **Achievements:** "Stick 1000 Knives", "Clear Stage 1 without missing".

## 4. Visual & Audio Polish
- **Advanced VFX:** Wood chips flying, screen shake on heavy hits, elaborate destruction animations.
- **Dynamic Audio:** Pitch shifting based on combo/speed.
- **Haptics:** Vibration on throw/hit (Mobile specific).

## 5. Technical/Quality of Life
- **Cloud Save:** Persist progress across devices.
- **Localization:** Support for multiple languages.
- **Accessibility:** Colorblind modes, adjustable text size.
